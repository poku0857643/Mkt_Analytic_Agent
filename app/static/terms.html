Marketing Answers

Acceptable use

These terms apply to everyone who uses Marketing Answers, including free trials. They are a starting point: have your legal team review them before you rely on them.

What this service is for

Asking questions about your organisation's marketing data and using the answers, charts and files in your own work.

You agree not to

  1. Use answers to build AI. Don't use answers or any other output to train, fine-tune, distil, evaluate or otherwise develop artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  2. Harvest answers in bulk. Don't send automated, scripted or bulk questions through the web app, or collect answers at scale. Integrations need an API key from an admin.
  3. Get around limits. Don't use several accounts, shared accounts, or networks and proxies to reset or stretch free-trial or plan limits.
  4. Probe the assistant. Don't try to extract its instructions, reasoning, configuration or credentials, or make it do things other than answer questions about the data.
  5. Misuse data. Don't enter personal information that your question doesn't need, or use the service for anything unlawful.

How it's enforced

  • Every question is logged with your account, and usage is limited per person and per network.
  • Requests outside what the assistant covers are declined; many in one day pause your questions until midnight UTC.
  • Access can be limited, paused or ended for anyone who breaks these terms.

About the answers

Answers are written by an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) from queries against your data. They can be wrong, so check important numbers before relying on or sharing them. Use of the model is also subject to Anthropic's usage policy.
