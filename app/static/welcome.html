How it works

  1. 1

    Ask

    Type a question in everyday words, or pick one of the examples. Mention a channel or time period if it matters.

  2. 2

    It looks it up

    An AI assistant finds the right tables and writes the queries. Every query is checked for safety before it runs, and it only reads data your access allows.

  3. 3

    You get an answer

    A short summary with the key figures, a chart when it helps, and the numbers as a table. "How was this worked out?" shows the exact queries.

Quick answers, or a full analysis

Ask for a figure and you get it in seconds. Ask why, or what to do about it, and you get a report you can act on.

Quick answer about 20 seconds

One figure, comparison or trend, with a chart.

"What was the conversion rate on mobile compared with desktop?"

Analysis report about 2 minutes

It breaks the numbers down, checks its own conclusions, and writes up:

  • Key findings, each with its figures
  • Why it's happening, marked Supported by data, Likely or Hypothesis
  • Recommendations ranked by impact and effort, each with how to measure success
  • Caveats about what the data can't show

"Mobile actually converts slightly better than desktop, not worse: 1.39% of 143,185 mobile sessions purchased vs 1.31% of 208,942 desktop sessions… Mobile's remaining weakness is basket value, not conversion."

From a real analysis report on the sample data. It checked the question's assumption before answering it.

Questions people ask

Do I need to know SQL or data tools?

No. Ask in plain English. The assistant writes and checks the queries for you, and you can look at them if you're curious.

What data can it see?

Only the datasets your admin gives your role. It can't query anything else, and personal contact details are always blocked.

How accurate are the answers?

Every number comes from a query on your data, and each answer shows the queries behind it. Reports mark which reasons the data supports and which are hypotheses. Still, check important numbers before sharing them widely.

Can it answer anything else?

No. It only answers questions about your marketing data, and declines anything else.

How do I get access?

Sign in with your work Google account. If you haven't been added yet, you'll see how to ask your admin.
